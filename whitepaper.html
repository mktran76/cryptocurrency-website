2. Problem Statement

Current blockchain networks face three fundamental challenges: scalability, cost, and user experience. Existing solutions offer partial fixes but no comprehensive answer to all three issues simultaneously.

  • High transaction fees during network congestion
  • Slow confirmation times hindering real-world adoption
  • Complex user interfaces deterring mainstream users
  • Fragmented liquidity across multiple chains

3. Our Solution

CryptoCoin introduces a novel Proof-of-Contribution consensus mechanism that rewards network participants based on their useful contributions to the ecosystem rather than raw computational power.
